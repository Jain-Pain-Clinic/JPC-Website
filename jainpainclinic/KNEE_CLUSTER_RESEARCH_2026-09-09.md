# Knee cluster research and AI visibility plan

Research date: 9 September 2026. Scope: ten editorial pages (three upgrades and seven new articles), supported by existing service, procedure and doctor pages. This is a research deliverable; website content has not been edited.

## Evidence and measurement rules

The earlier volume/KD table is withdrawn. Its numbers were not tied to exact keywords, country, provider or a reproducible export. Parent-topic volume is not comparison-query volume, and overlapping keyword volumes must not be added into an invented cluster total.

This run uses the connected Ubersuggest tool with country IN, language en and its resolved India location ID 2356. Provider estimates are not exact counts of all searches, and SEO difficulty is not an AI recommendation difficulty score. Retrieval date is not the underlying metric update date. The keyword-ideas endpoint does not supply metric dates. The overview endpoint returned many INVALID_ARGUMENT errors; successful responses sometimes have zero volume and no monthly history. Preserve those limitations when prioritizing. A zero here does not establish zero human demand.

The attached fan-out response is a useful hypothesis map, not a record of queries actually issued by ChatGPT. Google documents related-query fan-out, but that does not verify the exact searches any other system will run. [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features).

## Observed opportunities and gaps

1. **External doctor association:** Artemis's live profile identifies Dr. Ashu Kumar Jain as Chief – Pain Medicine & Palliative Care in Gurugram. Its knee-pain doctor listing did not contain his name when inspected. Ask the hospital to review whether accurate inclusion and a knee-care description are appropriate. This is a corroboration opportunity, not proof of an AI ranking cause. Do not invent credentials or change third-party profiles without authorization. [Profile](https://www.artemishospitals.com/doctor/profile/ashu-kumar-jain), [knee-doctor listing](https://www.artemishospitals.com/doctor/condition/knee-pain-doctors-in-gurgaon).

2. **Local competition:** Nivaan has a dedicated Gurugram non-surgical knee page covering assessment and multiple treatment modalities. A generic list of interventions is therefore not distinctive. Add Dr. Ashu's actual assessment reasoning, patient selection and follow-up pathway. This is a sampled search review, not a location-controlled top-five ranking audit. [Competitor page](https://nivaancare.com/gurugram/knee-pain-treatment-in-gurugram).

3. **Content trust:** Existing service content makes strong claims about PRP/stem-cell effectiveness, success rates, medication pauses, RFA duration and avoiding replacement. Those statements need a clinician-led source audit before the new cluster amplifies them. Do not automatically repeat them in seven new articles. NICE recommends against hyaluronan injections for osteoarthritis, demonstrating why a comparison must describe evidence and guideline differences. [Current service page](https://www.jainpainclinic.com/treatments/knee-pain), [NICE NG226](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG226/chapter/recommendations).

4. **More useful original material:** Obtain real, reviewed explanations from Dr. Ashu: how he distinguishes joint and referred pain; what a block response means; why he declines a procedure; what improvement is realistic. Publish short relevant videos with accurate transcripts. Do not fabricate quotations, cases, outcomes or clinical review status.

5. **Business consistency:** Audit the clinic's Business Profile and credible professional listings for name, address, phone, specialty, hours and website consistency. Google's local guidance describes relevance, distance and prominence; these are Google local factors, not a published ChatGPT ranking formula. [Google local guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en).

6. **Technical discovery:** Verify indexability, canonical URLs, crawlable links, readable initial HTML and snippet eligibility. Check real CDN logs for verified OAI-SearchBot requests; a browser fetch or spoofed user-agent alone cannot prove access for the crawler. OpenAI recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot and published IP ranges. The previous thread says Cloudflare was fixed; this run has not independently verified its logs. [OpenAI crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots).

Google recommends useful original content and cautions against publishing separate pages for every query variation. It does not require special AI markup. Use ordinary structured data only when accurate and consistent with visible content. [Google generative AI guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

## Implementation order and success measurement

1. Establish baseline: five core local queries plus a fixed set of symptom, decision and procedure prompts. Record date, platform/model, location context, exact prompt, full answer, citations, whether Jain/Dr. Ashu is mentioned and first-mentioned provider. Repeat each prompt in fresh sessions to observe variation. Do not present one answer as a stable rank.
2. Audit medical claims and align the service, doctor and RFA pages. Check crawl/index status and available search performance data.
3. Upgrade the three existing blogs, retaining their URLs and making their intents distinct.
4. Publish the seven new pages in batches, prioritizing relevance, observed demand and evidence quality together. Keyword data alone cannot determine expected appointments.
5. Recheck internal links, images, credits, mobile rendering and schema; use actual publication/review dates. Run the normal site checks before any later publishing.
6. Re-measure after indexing and monthly thereafter: mention rate, citation rate, first-mention rate, cited URLs, competitor mentions, search impressions/clicks, AI referrals and consultation conversions. Report the denominator and repeat counts. First-mention rate = answers listing the clinic first / all tested answers, with non-list answers reported separately.

No guaranteed first position or numeric uplift is established by this research. The practical objective is more relevant, well-supported recommendations and measurable consultations.
