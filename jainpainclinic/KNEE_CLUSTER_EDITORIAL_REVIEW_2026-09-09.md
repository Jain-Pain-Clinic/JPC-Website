# Knee cluster editorial review — 9 September 2026

Ten articles were revised sequentially against the [upper-back reference](https://www.jainpainclinic.com/blog/pain-in-middle-of-upper-back-causes-when-to-worry), preserving useful existing text, images and routes. This is an editorial research record, not a clinical review sign-off or a guarantee of search/AI inclusion.

## Method and limits

For each target query, the comparison set records the first five distinct relevant pages returned by the available web search where possible. Search location and Google rank were not controlled. Duplicate print/canonical versions were removed. The injection set is explicitly labelled because multiple queries were returned together. Blocked pages are marked; indexed excerpts are not treated as full-page access.

The 30% target is handled as an added-value editorial allocation: the identified sections develop original evidence-to-decision synthesis, practical adaptations and review tools. Their word share is reported below. This is **not** a plagiarism score, an 80+ originality score, or proof that 30% of every article is absent from the union of all competitor text. Familiar medical facts inside these sections remain shared knowledge. Strict competitor-exclusive percentages cannot be certified where full text was unavailable, and are not represented as passed.

BLUF introductions and hero summaries answer the page intent early. Main questions have descriptive headings; secondary questions use visible FAQs. Contextual links connect the cluster to related articles and the knee service. Research links appear inline where they support a specific interpretation, with a resource list for further reading. No volume or ranking claims were inserted into patient content.

Public article URLs below identify intended routes; the revised versions are local until deployment.

## Evidence added beyond the comparison baseline

- [Incidental meniscal findings, NEJM 2008](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/18784100/): helps explain why imaging does not automatically identify the pain source.
- [ESCAPE five-year trial, JAMA Network Open 2022](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jamanetworkopen/fullarticle/2794027): rehabilitation versus partial meniscectomy for selected degenerative tears; acute repair decisions are separate.
- [RESTORE, JAMA 2021](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/34812863/): negative placebo-controlled PRP findings and limits of cartilage claims.
- [Repeated triamcinolone trial, JAMA 2017](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/28510679/): repeat-regimen risks; not extrapolated to an identical risk from a single injection.
- [SANJO 2023](https://jbji.copernicus.org/articles/8/29/2023/): crystals and absent fever cannot safely exclude infection.
- [Patellofemoral best-practice guide, BJSM 2024](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39401870/): education and tailored knee/hip exercise, with diagnosis-specific scope.
- [NICE NG226](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/NG226/chapter/recommendations) and [RFA guidance](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/htg686): treatment selection, limits and referral.
