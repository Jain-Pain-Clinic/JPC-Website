## 2. Neck Pain Exercises and Stretches: What to Try, What to Avoid and When to Stop

**Reader task:** Know how to begin suitable movement, adjust it and recognise when self-directed exercise needs review.

**Primary candidate:** neck pain exercises.

**Secondary candidates:** exercises for neck pain, neck stretches, neck pain exercises at home, cervical pain exercises, desk-related neck pain.

**Slug:** `/blog/neck-pain-exercises-and-stretches`.

**SEO title:** Neck Pain Exercises: Safe Moves and When to Stop

**Meta description:** Explore gentle neck pain exercises, ways to adapt desk work, and signs to stop and seek advice. Learn when supervised physiotherapy may help.

**Planned sections, in order:**

1. Can exercise help neck pain? Define the intended audience and explain why one routine does not suit every cause.
2. Before starting: recent injury, neurological symptoms, previous surgery and individual restrictions.
3. Gentle neck rotation: starting position, comfortable range, slow technique and common errors.
4. Gentle side tilt: controlled movement without pulling the head into a stretch.
5. Comfortable neck flexion: how to keep the movement small and avoid forcing range.
6. Shoulder and upper-back movement: supporting the neck through a broader rehabilitation plan.
7. Strengthening progression: when a physiotherapist may introduce chin retraction, scapular work or isometrics; avoid treating these as universally suitable.
8. How much to do: source-supported beginner repetitions, gradual progression and a symptom check later that day/next morning. Exact doses will be verified during drafting.
9. When to reduce or stop: distinguish temporary local discomfort from spreading arm symptoms or new neurological changes.
10. Desk work, phone use and driving: movement breaks, screen placement and variation rather than a single “perfect” posture.
11. Why stretching alone may not be enough; how to judge progress by sleep, work tolerance and comfortable movement.
12. When supervised physiotherapy or a neck-pain assessment is appropriate.

**FAQs:** Can I exercise every day? Should I stretch if pain travels down my arm? Are neck circles helpful? Can exercises reverse cervical spondylosis? How soon should I notice improvement? What if exercises make symptoms worse?

**Original editorial contribution:** A simple exercise log recording movement, dose, symptoms during/after and next-day function. An exercise table will include purpose, technique, modification and stop rule.
